Introduction

I am Inhaan Bhano, a student of BS English Language Teaching at the University of Sindh, Jamshoro. For my school internship I was placed at Government Jamia Millia Campus High School in Tando Adam, where I worked with students and teachers from 14 September to 30 October 2026.

The first sessions were for watching: how teachers open a lesson, keep order and check understanding. After that I prepared lesson plans, ran pair and group tasks, asked questions to push students to think, and taught English lessons of my own.

This internship connected what I studied in class with a real classroom. It improved my communication, my planning and my confidence, and it showed me what the work of an English teacher actually looks like.

My Goals

What I wanted to achieve

My aim was to move from theory to practice and to learn how English is really taught in a government school. My objectives were:

  1. See how a government high school runs through the day.
  2. Plan lessons with a clear aim, an activity and a closing check.
  3. Teach English grammar, vocabulary, reading and writing in simple language.
  4. Explain difficult ideas with short examples.
  5. Notice that students learn at different speeds and support them.
  6. Use the board, textbook and real objects as teaching aids.
  7. Ask better questions and give useful feedback.
  8. Build punctuality, patience, responsibility and confidence.

I came back to these goals at the end of the internship to see how far I had come.
